## 1. PROJECT PURPOSE

BizVia is a GovTech platform for entrepreneurs and industrial businesses.

Problem:
Starting and operating a business requires multiple registrations, licences, NOCs, approvals, inspections, renewals and compliance actions. Requirements depend on business type, location, investment, project stage and other factors. Users often do not know exactly what approvals they need, what documents are required, what stage each application is at, or when action is required.

BizVia should provide one guided journey:

**Tell us what you are building → identify what approvals you need → prepare documents → submit/track applications → handle queries and inspections → monitor renewals and schemes.**

This is a prototype for SIH. Do NOT claim that it is connected to real government systems.

* Rounded cards
